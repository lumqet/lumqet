1. Who processes your data

Lumqet is a self-hosted application. Your data is processed on the server operated by the person or organisation who deployed the instance you are using, not by a separate analytics provider.

2. Data we collect

  • Panel account data — your email address and display name, used only to log you in to the panel.
  • Platform connection data — OAuth access and refresh tokens, the platform user identifier (open_id), display name and avatar URL of the account you connected. These are used to publish your scheduled posts.
  • Content you create — the text and media files of the posts you compose and schedule.
  • Operation logs — status messages about publish attempts (success, failure, retry) for troubleshooting.

3. Data we never collect

  • Your TikTok or YouTube password — the Service never asks for it.
  • Data about viewers, followers or any third party who watches your videos.
  • Payment data.

4. How data is used

Only to provide the Service: to authenticate you, to store your scheduled posts, and to publish them to the platform accounts you connected at the time you chose.

5. Sharing

Data is sent only to the platform whose account you connected (TikTok, YouTube), through that platform's official API, in order to publish your content. No data is sold or shared with advertisers.

6. Retention

Connection tokens are kept until you disconnect the account or delete it from the panel. Scheduled posts and logs are kept until you delete them.

7. Your rights

You can disconnect a platform account or delete it at any time from the panel, which removes its stored tokens. You can also delete your posts and media files.

8. Security

Tokens are stored server-side, panel sessions use secure httpOnly cookies, and media files are protected by authenticated or signed URLs.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do.
