Social media control panel

Lumqet

Lumqet is a self-hosted social media control panel where creators schedule and publish their own videos. It connects to TikTok and YouTube accounts with OAuth and publishes the videos you compose and schedule — nothing else.

Terms of Service Privacy Policy

What it does

  • Connect your own TikTok or YouTube account with OAuth (you approve the login yourself).
  • Write a post once, pick accounts, pick a date and time.
  • A built-in scheduler publishes the video at the chosen time.
  • Track which posts were published and their status.

What it does not do

  • It never asks for or stores your TikTok or YouTube password.
  • It never posts third-party or reposted content — only posts you composed yourself.
  • It does not collect data from people who watch your videos.

Scope of this website

This site hosts the public legal pages for the Lumqet application: its Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. The application itself is self-hosted and runs on the operator's own server.
